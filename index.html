Why Deux Maison

Cabin living in the most private part of Crosswinds

Deux Maison means "two homes" in French: chalet-style duetto homes with timber textures, sloped roofs and natural finishes, surrounded by over 35,000 pine trees. It sits in Lausanne, a 24-hectare enclave in the highest and most exclusive part of Crosswinds, with views of Tagaytay, Laguna de Bay and the Metro Manila skyline.
